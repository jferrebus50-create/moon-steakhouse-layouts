Layout Proposal 1
Crescent Bar + Round Dining Terrace
~20m × 20m
~3,000 sq ft
Amphitheater stairs run along the right side, going up toward the back-right corner — open to the sky, no roof over the seating. The crescent moon-shaped bar is tucked under the stairs on the left side where there's headroom, glowing chartreuse-yellow along its curved edge. At the top of the stairs, an enclosed room with solid canopy roof and rooftop lavender garden. The open terrace fills the left and center with round dining tables, ornamental trees, and glass railing for panoramic city views.

Layout Proposal 2
Crescent Bar + Communal Harvest Tables
~20m × 20m
~3,000 sq ft
Same stair orientation (right side, going up to the right) and crescent moon bar underneath on the left, but the terrace swaps to long communal harvest tables (seats 8-10 each) in two rows — better for group bookings and events. VIP room at the top of the stairs keeps its canopy roof and rooftop lavender garden. A small espresso/tapas bar station sits near the kitchen entry.
