Layout Proposal 1
Amphitheater + VIP Box + Open Terrace
20m × 20m
~3,000 sq ft
Diagonal amphitheater seating in vivid chartreuse-yellow and black, completely open-air under the sky — no roof over the tiers. The curved bar sits directly underneath the stairs. The enclosed VIP box (with its own solid canopy roof and lavender garden on top) sits beside the amphitheater. Open terrace dining fills the rest of the floor with round tables, ornamental trees, and full glass railing for the city view.
